# Sink — typography and motion

The approved foundation is an English, warm-paper portfolio with generous whitespace and original project media. This refinement gives the name a soft, expressive typographic voice without adding illustration clutter.

- Fraunces variable (OFL, locally hosted) is the signature/display face. Soft and wonky axes provide organic character; Archivo remains for reading and controls.
- The name is the focal interaction: subtle letter movement and a soft-axis change on hover; clicking crossfades between existing portraits.
- Project image and caption travel together in a 320 ms directional transition. No exit-then-enter blank interval. Drag has bounded resistance and no momentum, with arrow controls retained.
- Native project dialogs retain focus containment, Escape and focus restoration. Entry is 320 ms, exit 150 ms. Reduced motion removes spatial effects.
- Existing image and video files are untouched. Font license is in assets/fonts/Fraunces-OFL.txt.
